Next Month Action Plan

NOVEMBER 2026

01 · Scale LIVE Momentum

October LIVE GMV grew approximately 10.3% vs September based on four-week totals.

  • Audit creator slots, schedule and product pinning.
  • Reallocate prime-time slots to proven performers.
  • Test voucher/offer by creator and time slot.
  • Monitor GMV per stream, CTR and CTOR.
Suggested target: +10% vs October

02 · Scale Video Winners

October Video GMV grew approximately 3.8% vs September based on four-week totals.

  • Replicate winning hooks and product storytelling.
  • Refresh creatives with high impressions but weak conversion.
  • Build 7-day and 14-day post-performance review.
  • Measure GMV per video and orders per creator.
Suggested target: +8% video GMV

03 · Defend Hero Products

Top products account for a material share of affiliate sales.

  • Protect stock and price competitiveness for hero SKUs.
  • Build bundles and cross-sell angles.
  • Expand creator coverage for rising products.
Suggested target: +8% top-5 GMV

04 · Improve Creator Productivity

Creator volume should be evaluated with sales productivity.

  • Segment creators: scale, grow, maintain, reactivate.
  • Publish weekly leaderboard and deliverable tracking.
  • Set differentiated brief per creator tier.
Suggested target: +10% GMV per active creator

05 · MCN Governance

Compare agency GMV September–October and prioritize follow-up for MCNs with declining or incomplete baseline data.

  • Review GMV, paid vs settled orders and product sales coverage.
  • Scale agencies with consistent GMV growth and healthy settlement.
  • Build recovery plans for agencies with declining GMV.
Target: 100% monthly MCN reporting

06 · Data Quality & ROI

ROAS/ROI and some posted-date cohorts cannot be validated with current inputs.

  • Correct posted date/month mapping.
  • Add ad spend, affiliate cost and contribution margin.
  • Standardize weekly and monthly KPI definitions.
Target: complete source data before review
